## PROBLEM STATEMENT

Rural residents often struggle to find reliable service providers (plumbers, electricians, tutors) due to lack of centralized information and booking tools.

Existing methods are manual, word-of-mouth, or limited to urban-centric apps, leaving rural needs unmet.

---

## DESCRIPTION

Students will build a MERN stack web app where rural service providers can register, list their offerings, and manage bookings, while residents can search, review, and book providers. Features include authentication for providers and residents, service listings, booking calendar, reviews, and admin dashboards. This solution empowers rural communities with easy access to trusted services, improving convenience and livelihood.

---

---

## WHAT YOU'LL BUILD (MVP FEATURES)

### Service Provider Registration & Listings

Providers create accounts and list their services with details.

**Why:** Centralizes offerings for easy discovery by residents.

### Service Search & Filter

Residents search for services by category, rating, or location.

**Why:** Improves usability and relevance for rural users.

### Booking Calendar & Management

Residents book services; providers manage and confirm appointments.

**Why:** Simplifies scheduling and reduces missed appointments.

### Reviews & Ratings

Residents can leave reviews and ratings for providers.

**Why:** Builds trust and helps others find reliable services.

### Admin Dashboard

Admins oversee provider activities, bookings, and manage disputes.

**Why:** Ensures quality control and platform integrity.
